The scope is deliberately address planning and review evidence, not a full recreation of Infrahub's graph, branch model, automation engine, enterprise permissions or support service. No claim is made that Infrahub cannot answer the ten demonstrated questions. The custom offer is a version built around an operator's own records and rules.
